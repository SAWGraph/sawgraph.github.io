About Our Research

Our publications demonstrate the application of semantic web technologies and ontology-driven approaches to environmental data integration and analysis. Research areas include:

  • Ontology Design: Creating reusable ontologies for environmental contamination and sampling, facilities and industries, hydrology, spatial information, and chemical substances
  • Knowledge Graphs: Using interconnected knowledge graphs for integrated geospatial analysis and decision support
  • Contamination Monitoring: Supporting PFAS monitoring and analysis across heterogeneous environmental datasets
  • Spatial Reasoning: Developing precomputed approaches for efficient spatial relationship analysis
