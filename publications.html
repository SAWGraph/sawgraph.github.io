Research Areas

Together, these publications document several areas of SAWGraph research:

  • Ontology Design and Knowledge Representation: developing reusable semantic models for environmental contamination, facilities and industries, chemical substances, and related data
  • Knowledge Graph and Geospatial Analysis: connecting heterogeneous data through knowledge graphs and spatial relationships to support integrated analysis and decision-making
  • PFAS Monitoring and Transport: investigating contamination observations, environmental movement, and changes in PFAS concentrations across space and time
  • PFAS Fate and Soil Interactions: examining adsorption, mobility, and the relationships among PFAS properties, soil characteristics, and environmental behavior
