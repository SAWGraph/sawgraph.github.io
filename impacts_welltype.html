Testing Demo 2: PFAS Based on Well Type

Demo Question: Can well type predict PFAS contamination risk?

Use Case Area: Assessing and Mapping Impacts and Risks

This demo showcases how SAWGraph can be used to perform analyses to identify potential patterns in vulnerability to PFAS contamination. It shows how PFAS concentrations vary based on the type of well that was sampled, performs a statistical analysis, and visualizes the results based on the type of well a sample was likely taken from.

What This Demo Connects

Wells

Located water wells and their respective types in Maine.

Public and Private Water Supply Testing

PFAS test results from public water supply wells and private water supply wells.

Counties

Maine counties used to analyze, compare, and map data.

Demo Results

The map shows private water supply sample points, aquifers, and wells returned from the SAWGraph query based on the datasets currently represented in the graph. Results therefore reflect the scope and version of those datasets, as well as the conditions of the query.

Together, these results demonstrate how contamination observations can be connected with groundwater and well data to help identify locations where additional investigation or testing may be useful.

For this demonstration, nearby Maine Geological Survey aquifers have been combined into larger aquifer systems. The current aquifer information is limited and is being expanded.

The map is interactive. Pan and zoom to explore the results, use the layer controls to show or hide different types of features, and explore mapped features for additional information.

Wells Connected Through Aquifers

This map shows private water supply sample points with PFOA results above 200 ng/L, the aquifer systems associated with those sample locations, and Maine Geological Survey wells associated with those aquifer systems.

The available layers include contaminated sample points, aquifers, and wells.
